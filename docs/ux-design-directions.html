Question
Which technique is most effective for gathering requirements from large groups of stakeholders?
A. One-on-one interviews
B. Facilitated workshops
C. Document analysis
D. Observation
Reading Content

Facilitated Workshops are collaborative sessions where stakeholders work together to define requirements, resolve conflicts, and reach consensus.

They are particularly effective for large groups because they:
• Enable real-time collaboration and discussion
• Allow multiple perspectives to be heard simultaneously
• Build consensus more efficiently than individual interviews
